# Legacy route: grammatical-metaphor research

This filename is retained so older prompts and links continue to work. The repeated
theory and method material formerly kept here has moved to three canonical files:

- [gm-theory.md](gm-theory.md): Halliday's core re-mapping account, historical
  milestones, subtype boundaries, later research, and verified source routing;
- [gm-decision-protocol.md](gm-decision-protocol.md): context gate, congruent agnate,
  explicit cross-stratal mapping, counter-analysis, and graded decision;
- [gm-annotation-schema.md](gm-annotation-schema.md): Schema v3, JSON/JSONL/CSV,
  validation, provenance, and v2 compatibility.

Research methods, source audits, statistics, and publication reports remain prose or
tables unless they produce formal item-level coding. Only those records use Schema
v3, and every formal record must pass the bundled validator before return.

Do not cite this compatibility page as theory evidence. Reopen the primary book,
article, or presentation page named in [gm-theory.md](gm-theory.md) and follow
[source-citation-protocol.md](source-citation-protocol.md).

The migration preserves one critical distinction: semantic–lexicogrammatical
re-mapping is Halliday's core criterion. Semantic junction, MPP, and rank diagnostics
come from Wen Li and Bingjun Yang; FRP and Context-first/AS IF come from Bingjun
Yang's later work. These tools are also distinct from Yang Yanning's Chinese
descriptive framework.
